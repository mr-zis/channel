MR-ZIS produces short educational videos exploring everyday science, surprising facts, and questions about the world around us.

About MR-ZIS

Our content aims to make complex topics approachable through clear explanations, engaging narration, and original visual storytelling. Topics may include nature, the human body, technology, astronomy, and the relationship between scientific findings and religious texts. Scientific evidence and religious interpretation are distinguished where relevant.

About the publishing workflow

MR-ZIS uses a creator-operated workflow to prepare videos and related descriptions for publication on supported platforms. Where TikTok account access is enabled, the account owner authorizes access through TikTok's official authentication process. The workflow is not a public video-sharing service or a platform for other users to submit content.
