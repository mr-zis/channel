Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MR-ZIS handles information in connection with its website and creator-operated video publishing workflow. The workflow is intended for its operator and authorized account owners, not general public registration.

1. Information handled

When an account owner authorizes TikTok integration, the workflow may handle authentication tokens, account identifiers, account information returned under granted permissions, and information needed to prepare or track video uploads. It also processes video files, captions, publication settings, and operational logs. The exact information depends on the permissions and features actually enabled.

2. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate authorized accounts, prepare or submit content as instructed, check publication status where supported, troubleshoot errors, and maintain the security of the workflow. MR-ZIS does not use TikTok account information to sell personal data or serve targeted advertising.

3. Storage and security

The publishing workflow is designed to operate on a computer controlled by the operator. Authentication credentials and tokens must not be placed in public website files or source repositories. Reasonable access restrictions and secure storage should be used. The public GitHub Pages website does not itself collect TikTok credentials or perform OAuth token exchanges.

Operator confirmation required: Before publishing this policy, verify the actual token storage method, logging, retention, and any third-party services used by the TikTok integration. Update this section to match the implementation.

4. Sharing and third parties

Information necessary for authorized publication is sent to TikTok through its official interfaces. Video content and metadata may also be sent to other publishing platforms when separately authorized. Service providers may process information only as needed to provide their services, if used. TikTok's own privacy policy governs information processed by TikTok.

5. Retention and deletion

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for authorized publishing, troubleshooting, and applicable legal obligations. Account owners can revoke MR-ZIS access through TikTok's connected-app settings. To request deletion of locally stored account information or tokens, contact [INSERT CONTACT EMAIL]. Revoking platform access does not automatically delete every local log or already published video; deletion requests will be handled as applicable.

6. Website data

This website is a static informational site hosted on GitHub Pages. MR-ZIS does not intentionally include its own analytics trackers, advertising pixels, or contact forms. GitHub, as the hosting provider, may process technical connection information under its own policies.

7. Rights and questions

Depending on applicable law, individuals may have rights to request access, correction, or deletion of their personal information. Contact [INSERT CONTACT EMAIL] to make a request. We may update this policy when the workflow or data practices change.
